the one you've already got

Caring

There's a framework behind this page with four gears in it. Here's the important bit up front: you're already flat out good at one of them, and it happens to be the hardest one.

Being the bloke people actually want around can't be faked, can't be automated, and can't be bought. A machine will out-think you all day. It cannot turn up when someone's car dies. So this gear isn't homework — it's the thing to lean on while the other three catch up.

Shift the thinking
  • Information keeps getting cheaper. People you can count on keep getting rarer. You're holding the scarce one.
  • Half the people reading a page like this would trade what they've got for what you've got here.
  • Showing up is a skill. It's just one nobody hands out certificates for.
Do it now
Two minutes
Text a mate something that isn't a meme.
This week
Check on the one who's gone quiet lately.
Whenever
Ask how someone is, then wait for the second answer. The first one's always "yeah, good".
poke it once before you write it off

Curious

Not school curious. Smaller than that. It's just — before you decide something's not for you, have one poke at it.

Shift the thinking
  • The question you reckon is dumb is usually the one everyone else was too proud to ask.
  • When this gear seizes up it doesn't make you thick. It makes everything pre-decided, and nothing new gets in.
  • People love being asked how they ended up doing what they do. Almost nobody asks them.
Do it now
Tomorrow
Take a different way home.
Five minutes
Look up the one thing you've wondered about for years and never bothered with.
Next chat
Ask someone how they actually got into what they do. Then shut up and listen.
something with a before and an after

Constructive

This isn't about productivity. It's that unfinished things sit in your head as a low hum, and finished things — even tiny stupid ones — clear a bit of it out.

Shift the thinking
  • A big plan is just an unfinished thing that gets heavier every day you don't start it.
  • Ten minutes washing the car does more for your Thursday than a five-year plan written on Sunday.
  • Small completions beat big intentions. Every single time. It's not close.
Do it now
Ten minutes
Fix the thing that's annoyed you for a month.
Right now
Chuck out one thing you're never going to use.
Anytime
Show someone how to do the thing you're good at.
stopping counts as doing something

Chill

Most misunderstood one, because the word suggests the couch. It isn't the couch, and it definitely isn't scrolling — scrolling is stimulation, not rest, and your body knows the difference even when you don't.

Shift the thinking
  • This is the gear that makes the other three possible. It's not the reward at the end of the list.
  • Caring runs out if you never refill it. That's not weakness, that's just how it works.
  • Sleep is the cheapest one on this whole page and the one everyone skips.
Do it now
Ten minutes
Go outside and leave your phone inside.
Tonight
Go to bed an hour before you were going to.
Before you go in
Sit in the car for five minutes. Nothing on.
the honest version

The thing on your phone

Everyone's banging on about AI and most of it is rubbish, so here it is straight. It's a thing you type at. It types back. It's not a person and it's not your mate, and it won't fix anything on its own.

What it is: free, there at 3am, doesn't get bored, doesn't tell anyone, and has no idea what you did last year. That last part matters more than people admit.

Shift the thinking
  • There's a whole category of question people never ask anyone — not because it's shameful, but because asking means admitting you didn't already know.
  • Give those to the machine. It frees the humans up for the stuff only humans are any good at.
  • Don't test it with something clever. Test it with something you actually want to know.
Try one of these
I've got twenty bucks and half a fridge. What can I cook?
Help me write a message to someone I've been avoiding.
My car's making a noise like this. Where do I start?
I can't sleep. Talk to me about something boring.
What would it take to [the thing you keep saying you'll do]?
